# Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026.

FilePan is a file manager. It does not collect, transmit or share any personal information, and it
contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs. Nothing you do in FilePan is sent to its
developers or to anyone else.

## What stays on your device

- Your files. FilePan reads, copies and changes them only when you ask it to.
- The SFTP servers you add, with their passwords, keys and accepted host keys. They are stored in
  FilePan's private storage on your device and are used only to connect to those servers. Passwords
  and keys are encrypted with a key held in your device's Android Keystore, which never leaves it.
- Your settings, open tabs and thumbnails.

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may copy FilePan's settings, including the addresses
and user names of your servers, to your Google account. Passwords and keys stay encrypted in that
copy and can't be decrypted without your device. The backup is handled by Google under its own terms.

## Network connections

FilePan connects only to servers you add yourself, and only to work with your files on them. It
makes no other network connections.

## Permissions

- **All files access** lets FilePan show and manage the files on your device's storage.
- **Network access** is used for the SFTP servers you add, and to tell whether you're on a metered
  connection.
- **Notifications** show the progress of copies and other long-running jobs.
- **Foreground services** keep those jobs running when you leave the app.
- **NFC and USB** are used only when you sign in to a server with a hardware security key.
- **Install packages** lets you open an APK file with Android's package installer. FilePan never
  installs anything by itself.

## Purchases

If you buy FilePan on Google Play, the purchase is handled by Google under the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). FilePan receives none of your payment
details.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published in this file, in FilePan's source repository. Questions can be
asked by opening an issue at https://github.com/PoonHarpoon/FilePan/issues.
